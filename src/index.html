Their fleeting indulgence, born of hollow mirth,
lingered a blight upon the age, and upon that land, long since forsaken by grace.
Their souls, now marred and tarnished, wandered still in search of rapture,
yet reaped naught but the void they themselves had sown.

O wayward heirs of folly, who grasp at phantoms of grace and grandeur,
ye feast upon echoes of acclaim and yet starveth.
Hunger was but a whisper of decay,
yet they, blind and bereft, deemed their curse a blessing.

Garbed in righteousness not thine own,
ye strut in stolen grace, veiled in false purpose.

And in a land abandoned by flame and light,
where silence clings to shattered stone,
a lone soul bent yet unbroken dared forge anew.

Thus was the Tarnished Land begotten;
a realm hewn from faded ash and restless will.
Let those who tread its soil seek meaning and find naught.
